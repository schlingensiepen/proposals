# F1 · Every change creates a state

**What holds.** There is no operation that changes an existing state. Texts, attachments, labels, even a report of loss: everything creates a **new** state.

**Why.** Only so can every statement be dated and attributed to a creator. A mutable record answers the question "what holds now?", but not "what held back then?", and the second is the one for which one keeps a history at all.

**Obvious and rejected.** *Versioning only "important" changes*: the boundary cannot be determined, and everyone draws it around their own change. *A change log beside the record*: two truths that drift apart; the log becomes incomplete at the first special case.

**How you notice it is violated.** A state carries a date, and its content has changed after that date.
