# Rules

Eight rules. F1 is the separation itself. F2 fixes the direction. F3 governs the handover between the two. F4 and F5 are the two forms with their conditions; F5 is the answer to a tool limit that occurred in practice. F6 concerns the duty to announce in shared work. F7 gives the handover its review, F8 records every release. F1 carries everything; F4 and F5 are interchangeable without the rest falling.

* [F1 Two repositories, two histories](./rules/F1-two-repositories-two-histories.md) - Genesis and product lie in separate repositories; the work history is a diary, the product history a publication history; neither is smoothed nor bloated.
* [F2 The work embeds the product, never the reverse](./rules/F2-the-work-embeds-the-product.md) - The work repository embeds the product repository; the product knows nothing of the workshop and contains no reference to it.
* [F3 The handover is deliberate and copies instead of linking](./rules/F3-the-handover-is-deliberate-and-copies.md) - Nothing reaches the product in passing; what is taken over carries its statement and its reason; the product is understandable without the workshop; what was taken over is generated anew, not updated piecemeal.
* [F4 Submodule form](./rules/F4-submodule-form.md) - Where the tools allow it, the product repository is a submodule of the work repository, which records the product state of every working state.
* [F5 Two-roots form](./rules/F5-two-roots-form.md) - Where the tools can only write files, two independent roots with an ignore rule; the connection is written out instead of recorded.
* [F6 Writing into another's repository only with announcement](./rules/F6-writing-into-another-repository-only-with-announcement.md) - An explicit rule for who may write where without asking; free in one's own workshop, elsewhere only after announcement.
* [F7 A review is offered before every handover](./rules/F7-a-review-is-offered-before-every-handover.md) - Offered emphatically or as a standing approval; two reviewers of other models than the builder; at most three rounds, then a convergence report, never a silent approval.
* [F8 Every release names its state and its purpose](./rules/F8-every-release-names-its-state-and-purpose.md) - "State x of the artifact, released for purpose Z", bound to a frozen state; a decision in the workshop, a release tag in the product.
