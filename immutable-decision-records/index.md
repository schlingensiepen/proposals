# Rules

One answer each; give one up and you are no longer using the concept. F2 carries everything, F1 is its precondition, F3 to F6 govern what a replacement looks like, F7 and F8 stand independently.

* [F1 One record, one decision](./rules/F1-one-record-one-decision.md) - Every decision lives in its own file with its own sequential number; there is no collection file.
* [F2 After acceptance the content is immutable](./rules/F2-content-immutable-after-acceptance.md) - Statement, rationale, options and consequences are frozen; only the head and the supersession notice at the opening stay editable, plus typos and dead links.
* [F3 Replacement is complete](./rules/F3-replacement-is-complete.md) - A replacing record answers the question completely afresh; there is no "partly superseded".
* [F4 The superseded record stays](./rules/F4-superseded-record-stays.md) - Not deleted, emptied, renamed or moved; it keeps its own title and gets a status marker and a pointer to its successor.
* [F5 The replacement relation is n:m and kept at both ends](./rules/F5-relation-n-to-m-at-both-ends.md) - Two lists in every head, what it supersedes and what superseded it, maintained in one move.
* [F6 Numbers are never reused](./rules/F6-numbers-are-never-reused.md) - A number stays with its record, even a discarded draft; gaps are not closed.
* [F7 The rejected alternatives are mandatory](./rules/F7-rejected-alternatives-are-mandatory.md) - Every record names the options seriously considered and why each did not win.
* [F8 An index shows the standing](./rules/F8-an-index-shows-the-standing.md) - One overview shows for every record whether it applies or is superseded and by what, updated with every new record.
