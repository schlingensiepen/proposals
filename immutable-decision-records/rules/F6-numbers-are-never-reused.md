# F6 · Numbers are never reused

**Applies to:** author.

**What holds.** A number once issued stays with its record, even if that record was discarded while still a draft. Gaps in the sequence are normal and are not closed.

**Why.** Numbers get cited: in meetings, in source comments, in other documents, in mail. A reused number makes every earlier citation silently wrong: the pointer still resolves to an existing record, just the wrong one. A dead pointer is harmless; a redirected one is dangerous.

**Considered and rejected.** *Close gaps so the sequence looks clean*: cosmetic, and it usually shifts several numbers at once. *Drop numbers altogether and cite by title*: titles grow long, change along the chain (F4), and cannot be shortened in speech.

**How you notice it is violated.** An older pointer leads to a record that plainly discusses something else.
