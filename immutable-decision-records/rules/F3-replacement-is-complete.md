# F3 · Replacement is complete, there is no "partly superseded"

**Applies to:** author.

**What holds.** A replacing record answers the question completely afresh, including the parts that remain unchanged. A marker of the kind "still applies, except paragraph 3" is not permitted.

**Why.** Whoever reads "partly superseded" must hold two records in mind at once and decide for themselves which sentence is hit by what. That is exactly the work this concept means to take off the reader, and it is error-prone work, performed anew by every reader, each time possibly with a different result. The writer performs it once.

**Obvious and rejected.** *A delta record describing only the change*: saves effort in writing and costs it in every reading; after the second revision you need three records to answer one question. *A pointer saying "otherwise the predecessor applies"*: the same trap in polite form: it makes the new record incomplete, and when reading the chain nobody can tell which version of "the rest" was meant.

**How you notice it is violated.** A question can only be answered by laying two or more records side by side.
